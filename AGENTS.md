- Seasonal scheduling: all route/calendar/status due-day checks go through `src/lib/service-season.ts` (`isServiceWeek`); why: one source of truth, billing stays independent.
- Billing: every money table carries business_id; issuing/voiding invoices and recording payments only via SECURITY DEFINER RPCs (billing_issue_invoice, billing_void_invoice, billing_record_payment) that write ledger_entries + billing_audit_log; amounts in integer cents; why: immutable books and multi-business separation.
- Invoice delivery: channel resolution, schedule and roster proposals live in `src/lib/invoice-delivery.ts`; preferences change only via set_my_invoice_channel / admin_set_invoice_channel RPCs (audited, STOP-list checked); billing_run_items idempotency_key is unique and clients can only set pending/approved/excluded; why: STOP and consent always win, no double sends, sending stays server-side.
- Broadcast SMS: recipients are resolved and sent only server-side in `send-sms-broadcast` (admin-checked, confirm + matching expected count required); opt-outs live in `sms_opt_outs` keyed by E.164 and are honored by broadcasts; why: client can't widen audience or bypass consent.
- Ideal chemistry targets (chart, calculator, CYA/calcium dosing) all come from `src/lib/ideal-chemistry.ts`, with per-pool overrides in `clients.chemistry_targets`; why: screens can never disagree.
- Chemistry Lab is a controlled presentation of parent-owned readings; visual scales/status/advice live in `src/lib/chemistry-lab.ts` and use ideal-chemistry ranges and existing dose engines; why: voice, manual entry, reports and recommendations stay synchronized without a second save path.
- Dosing: all amounts come from src/lib/pool-chemistry.ts and src/lib/cya-calcium-dosing.ts with stoichiometry-checked constants; unknown gallons/strength never yield an amount and pH gets no precise dose; why: chemical safety.
- Phosphates (ppb PO4) and iron/copper (ppm) rules live in `src/lib/phosphate-metals.ts`; remover/sequestrant amounts only from the product label dose + gallons + context, never from a reading alone, and phosphates never feed sanitizer/health status; why: chemical safety.
- Calcium chloride rates come from PURE_CACL2 / label strength in `cya-calcium-dosing.ts`; unconfirmed bags assume the business's default flake product as a labelled estimate; why: label-dependent strength.
- Chemical product strength and pool suitability come from `chemical_product_labels` (admin-verified, read via `src/lib/product-labels.ts`); doses never use a per-device tick or guessed strength; why: saved label facts, not guesses.
- QuickBooks Online: OAuth tokens live only in service-role tables (qbo_connections, qbo_oauth_states) and are used only by the admin-checked qbo-connect edge function; every Intuit call goes through `supabase/functions/_shared/qbo-oauth.ts` (discovery endpoints, transient-only retries, intuit_tid, sanitized `qbo_error_logs`); read-only until guarded writes are approved; why: tokens never reach the browser, errors are support-traceable, and QBO stays the accounting source of truth.
- QuickBooks environments: sandbox and production each use their own keys (QBO_SANDBOX_*/QBO_PRODUCTION_*, legacy QBO_CLIENT_* = sandbox only), and every qbo_connections read/write, cleanup and API host is scoped to the active QBO_ENVIRONMENT; production fails closed without its keys; why: tokens, rows and URLs can never cross environments.
